Executive Speaker Bio

Institutional profile

Ready to use in congress programs and scientific committees.

Carlos Augusto Gómez Ponce is a board-certified infectious diseases physician and pediatrician with over nine years of clinical, academic, and public health leadership on Mexico's northern border in Ciudad Juárez. He serves as the clinical lead of the HIV care cohort (~980 patients) at IMSS General Hospital No. 35, pediatric infectious diseases consultant at the Women's Hospital and the Children's Specialty Hospital (where he co-leads the Antimicrobial Stewardship committees and serves on Infection Prevention), and adjunct lecturer in Infectious Diseases at the Autonomous University of Ciudad Juárez (UACJ).

A medical graduate of the Benemérita Universidad Autónoma de Puebla (BUAP), he trained in Pediatrics at the Autonomous University of Chihuahua and in Infectious Diseases at the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM) / Hospital Infantil de México Federico Gómez. He also holds a Master's degree in Public Health Leadership from Universidad Anáhuac and completed advanced pediatric HIV training through Fondazione Penta (PentaTr@ining 2026, Padua, Italy). His academic and policy work spans HIV care cascades, prevention of mother-to-child transmission of HIV and syphilis, viral hepatitis elimination, antimicrobial stewardship in resource-limited settings, and emerging infectious diseases in migrant populations.
